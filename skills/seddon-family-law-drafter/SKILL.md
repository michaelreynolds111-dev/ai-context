## Hard rules
- **Every factual assertion in an affidavit must be based on something the deponent personally knows.** Flag hearsay clearly with [HEARSAY — consider].
- **Never fabricate dates, amounts, or events.** If a fact is uncertain, write it as uncertain: "on or about [date]", "approximately $[amount]".
- **Kennon argument:** financial abuse and coercive control can be a contribution. State the conduct, its financial effect, and the nexus — do not overreach.
- **Family violence context is always present in this matter.** Drafting should reflect this without editorialising. Facts speak; let them.
- **Cite the financial forensics** — every dollar figure in an affidavit should be traceable to the Seddon financial forensics analysis or a named source document.
- **Form 13 is strict.** Follow the current prescribed form exactly. Do not improvise structure.
