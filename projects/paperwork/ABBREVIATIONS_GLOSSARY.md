# Service Abbreviations Glossary

**Purpose:** Reference card for the Paperwork agent. Service abbreviations are
used as-is in all documents (never translated or expanded). This glossary
provides the meaning of each abbreviation so the agent understands what it's
referencing when it appears in source material.

**Classification:** [SENSITIVE] — references youth mental health service
structures. Sanitized — no client data.

---

## Clinical Service Abbreviations

| Abbreviation | Meaning | Context |
|---|---|---|
| YMHWS | Youth Mental Health and Wellbeing Service | The service the clinician works for — the writer's service |
| CTM | Clinical Team Meeting | Weekly meeting with the psychiatrist where CASP4 formulations are presented |
| MARAM | Multi-Agency Risk Assessment and Management | The Victorian risk assessment framework used across family violence and mental health services |
| IVO | Intervention Order | A court order protecting a person from family violence (formerly AVO in other states) |
| NDIS | National Disability Insurance Scheme | Federal disability support scheme — provides funding for supports, assessments, and services |
| NDIA | National Disability Insurance Agency | The agency that administers the NDIS |
| MHCP | Mental Health Care Plan | A GP-issued plan enabling access to subsidised mental health services |
| AOD | Alcohol and Other Drugs | The drug and alcohol service sector |
| FV | Family Violence | Used in compound terms: FV safety plan, FVISS |
| FVISS | Family Violence Information Sharing Scheme | A Victorian scheme allowing services to request police information about family violence risk |
| CNV | Community Navigation Volunteer (context-dependent) | A support service |
| L17 | Local 17 | A police report type — a family violence incident report |
| CASA | Centre Against Sexual Assault | Specialist sexual assault counselling and support service |
| DASH | Domestic Abuse, Stalking and Harassment | A risk assessment tool used in some services |

## Youth Mental Health Service Abbreviations

| Abbreviation | Meaning | Context |
|---|---|---|
| YPARC | Youth Prevention and Recovery Care | A residential youth mental health service providing short-term support during crisis |
| WYPARC | Women's Youth Prevention and Recovery Care | A YPARC service specifically for young women |
| YCMHT | Youth Community Mental Health Team | A community-based youth mental health team |

## Clinical Role Abbreviations

| Abbreviation | Meaning | Context |
|---|---|---|
| LC | The clinician (writer's initials — varies by clinician) | Used in medication blocks and clinical notes |
| GP | General Practitioner | The client's local doctor |
| Dr [Surname] | Psychiatrist or medical doctor | The psychiatrist at CTM or the client's treating doctor |

## Medication Abbreviations

| Abbreviation | Meaning | Context |
|---|---|---|
| mg | Milligrams | Dose unit |
| nocte | At night | Dosing instruction |
| mane | In the morning | Dosing instruction |
| PRN | Pro re nata (as needed) | Medication taken as required, not on a fixed schedule |
| IR | Immediate Release | A formulation of medication that releases immediately (vs extended release) |
| depot | Depot injection | A long-acting injectable form of medication (typically antipsychotic) |
| ECG | Electrocardiogram | Heart monitoring — required for some medications |

## Usage Rules

- These abbreviations are used **as-is** in all documents. Never expand them in
  the output.
- When a source document uses an abbreviation not on this list, use it as-is
  and flag it for the clinician to confirm.
- The agent does not need to know the full meaning to draft correctly — the
  glossary exists so the agent understands the service context, not so it can
  explain terms to the reader.
